UIU CSE Fest 2025

Event Date: January 17-18, 2025

Guest Speakers: Chief Guest: Dr. Muhammad Fouzul Kabir Khan, Honorable Advisor, Ministry of Power, Energy, and Mineral Resources, Special Guest: Shish Haider Chowdhury, Secretary, Information and Communication Division, Ministry of Posts, Telecommunication, and Information Technology, Chief Patron: Prof. Dr. Md. Abul Kashem Mia, Vice-Chancellor, UIU.

Event Summary: UIU CSE Fest 2025 was a national-level technology festival organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at United International University, featuring programming, blockchain, ICT, project showcasing, and robotics competitions. As an Organizer and member of the Event & Logistics Team, I contributed to event planning, coordination, logistics management, and the smooth execution of various activities throughout the fest. The event brought together around 1,500+ students from approximately 50+ public and private universities and 30+ colleges across Bangladesh. It featured competitions and showcases including the UIU Inter-University Programming Contest (IUPC), Inter-University Blockchain Olympiad, ICT Olympiad, Project Show, Line Following Robot (LFR), and Robo Soccer, fostering technological innovation, creativity, and competitive problem-solving.

The event brought together students, innovators, academic leaders, government representatives, and industry experts to promote innovation and foster the next generation of entrepreneurs.

The Pathao AIM Internship 2024 Roadshow

Event Date: November 13, 2024

Guest Speakers: Dr. Suman Ahmmed, Associate Professor and Director, CDIP, UIU, Fariha Ahmed, A Rahman Ovi, Anamika Zaman Annesha.

Event Summary: Pathao AIM Internship 2024 Roadshow was a career and recruitment-focused event organized by the Center for Development of IT Professionals (CDIP) and UIU App Forum at United International University. A quiz competition was also arranged, where participants competed for exciting prizes, with the top scorer being shortlisted for a direct interview for the Pathao AIM Internship.
